07 · Discipline

Do & Don't

The system lives on restraint. Glow is the first casualty when it's overused.

● Do

  • Let the dark base breathe — panels are cutouts in darkness, not stacked boxes.
  • Use primary magenta sparingly — brand, focus, one key action per frame.
  • Reserve cyan for live/healthy, yellow for waiting/warning. Consistency is the whole point.
  • Track out uppercase labels in font-label. It's the connective tissue.
  • Fade non-hero surfaces. Elevate only when active.

✕ Don't

  • Don't paint large surfaces in primary or secondary — they're accents, not fills.
  • Don't stack multiple glows. Text glow or border glow, never both.
  • Don't use pure #fff — use foreground (slate-200).
  • Don't introduce new brand colors without adding them as tokens first.
  • Don't hardcode hex values in components. Tokens or nothing.
